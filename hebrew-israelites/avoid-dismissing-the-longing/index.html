The verdict

Do not dismiss this. Answer the longing, and say what the church owes.

Avoid dismissal, because the longing is real. The movement grew exactly where the church left a hole. American churches were silent about slavery when they should have spoken, and many of them blessed it outright.

People did not join because the 12 Tribes Chart is convincing. They joined because someone finally told them their suffering had a place in God's story, and that they were somebody. That is a real need, and the gospel meets it more fully than the chart does.

So affirm the question. Name the failure honestly, including the churches that blessed slavery. Then show what adoption in Christ gives by grace. It gives what the genealogy could not deliver even if it were true: a name from God, an inheritance, and a Father. End the section here, not on a rebuttal.
