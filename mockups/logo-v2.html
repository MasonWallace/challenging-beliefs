Size and position fix

You're right that it sits small and high. The hero previews above already use the corrected values: the mark goes from 42px to 70px, and the gap under it from 16px to 26px, with more breathing room above.

CSS change on the homepage: .scale{font-size:2.6rem} → 4.4rem, header{padding:9vh 0 46px} → 11vh 0 52px, h1{margin:16px 0 0} → 26px 0 0. Mobile scales down proportionally.

My read

For a site whose whole argument is "go look at the text", A (the plain open book) is the honest one, and dropping the circle bought back enough room that it now survives a 16px tab on its own. B (light through the pages) is what I'd choose if you want warmth without decoration — the rays are plain strokes, so unlike D and E it does not fall apart when it shrinks. F (solid) is the crispest favicon of the six but says the least at hero size.

D and E are hero-only. Their glow and beam are gradients, and gradients vanish below about 24px — if you pick one of those, the favicon has to be A or F wearing the same colours.
