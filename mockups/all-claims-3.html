Option A
A picture beside every claim

A thumbnail turns a line of text into a thing you look at. The catch is real: only 41% of Mormonism cases and 40% of Islam cases have an image, and JW, Hebrew Israelites, Messiah and Case for God have none at all. The grey letter blocks below are what an image-less row looks like — that is 70 of 119 rows today.
