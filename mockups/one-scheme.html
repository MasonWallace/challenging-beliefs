One colour scheme

Four palettes with actual hue in the surfaces — no near-black, no near-white, and no light/dark pair. Each is a single scheme for the whole site. Shown on a real slice: hero, section cards, hub sidebar, claim rows, all four verdict badges, and a caption.

Every colour below is verified: body text is 11:1 or better, and muted text, captions, links and all four verdict badges clear 4.5:1. One honest tradeoff — putting colour in the background costs you the extreme 16:1 contrast of black-on-white. These land near 11:1, which is still well above the 7:1 AAA standard.

One consequence to decide on. Your six hub pages have a working Light / Dark button, and the article pages follow the reader's system setting. A single committed scheme means that button goes away and those prefers-color-scheme blocks come out. That's the point of picking one — but it is a real feature being removed, so say the word before I do it.
