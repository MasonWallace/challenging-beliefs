Seventy-two wives per martyr — Tirmidhi 1663

Admitted

The facts sit in Islam's own trusted sources. You can make this point from their books alone.

How the Quran describes paradise

It is insistently physical. Wide-eyed maidens "whom no man or jinn has touched" (Quran 55:56, 74).

Virgins made devoted and of equal age (56:35-38). Kawa'ib atraban, rendered full-breasted maidens of equal age (78:33).

Wine, couches, and boys who serve.

What the hadith add

The martyr is "married to seventy-two wives among the houris" (Jami at-Tirmidhi 1663, which Tirmidhi graded hasan sahih). Sahih al-Bukhari 3243 describes wives whose marrow is visible through their flesh, in a pearl tent sixty miles wide.

IslamQA 60188 affirms the houris as literal wives for sexual enjoyment.

What Jesus said

In the resurrection "they neither marry nor are given in marriage" (Matthew 22:30). Heaven's centre is God himself dwelling with his people (Revelation 21:3; 22:4).

Psalm 16:11 says the fullness of joy is in his presence.

The Muslim answer, at full strength

IslamQA 60188 replies that critics caricature paradise. The Quran itself says "the pleasure of Allah is greater" than all of it (9:72), and sound hadith make seeing Allah's face the supreme reward.

Physical delights are real because people are raised bodily — a point Christians share, with a wedding feast, a city and a renewed earth. The 72 number rests on a single chain some scholars grade lower.

And kawa'ib is a contested translation. Believing wives are promised higher status than the houris.

How strong is this argument?

They admit the substance themselves. IslamQA and classical commentary teach literal houris, and Tirmidhi graded the 72 report hasan sahih.

The 9:72 reply is fair as far as it goes. But the repeated picture of reward is virgins, couches and wine, while God's own presence with his people — the engine of hope from Eden to Revelation — sits at the edge.

Compare the goals, not the caricature.

What to say

"What is the best thing about heaven, in your view? For me it is God himself."

How they answer this — at its strongest

They say Allah's pleasure is the greater reward, and bodies are raised whole.

IslamQA 60188 replies that critics caricature paradise. The Quran itself says the pleasure of Allah is greater than all of it (9:72), and sound hadith make the sight of Allah's face the supreme reward.

Physical delights are real because people are raised bodily. Christianity shares that point, with a wedding feast, a city and a renewed earth. And they are cleansed of sin, since there is no idle talk therein (56:25).

The number 72 comes from a single companion-chain that some scholars grade lower. Renderings like full-breasted for kawa'ib are contested. And believing wives, they note, are promised higher standing than the houris.

The verdict

Strong for you: compare the goals, not the caricature.

This is graded admitted because mainstream Islamic authority affirms the substance. IslamQA and classical tafsir teach literal houris as sexual companions. And Tirmidhi himself graded the 72-wives hadith hasan sahih, that is, good and sound.

The 9:72 reply is fair as far as it goes, since Islam does hold Allah's pleasure supreme. But it does not erase what the Quran repeats. Its dominant picture of reward is virgins, couches and wine, while God's own presence with his people sits at the edge. That presence is the engine of biblical hope from Eden to Revelation. The point for evangelism stands on Islam's own affirmed doctrine, not on exaggeration.
