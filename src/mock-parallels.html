The same pattern, again and again

Four movements, born centuries apart, that keep arriving at the same handful of structures.

1 · One man, one angel, no witnesses

A new revelation that corrects the Bible arrives privately, to one individual, with no second witness.

Mormonism

Moroni appears to Joseph Smith alone — and the earliest accounts name the angel Nephi.

Islam

Gabriel appears to Muhammad alone at Hira; he is terrified and doubts the source.

Jehovah's Witnesses

No angel — but an invisible 1919 inspection with equally no witnesses.

Hebrew Israelites

Not this pattern — no founding revelation claim.

2 · The Bible had to be broken first

Before a new authority is accepted, the existing Scriptures must be shown to be corrupt or insufficient.

Mormonism

"Plain and precious things" removed — and a corrected Bible the church never adopted.

Islam

Tahrif — though textual corruption is a late doctrine.

Jehovah's Witnesses

An anonymous committee produces the organization's own Bible.

Hebrew Israelites

The KJV kept, but read by a method that lets any chapter mean anything.

…four more patterns follow, then the Galatians 1:8 test and the how-to-use rules.

C · One pattern at a timeScroll-paced, editorial Most readable · slowest · the cross-movement comparison is weaker

The same pattern, again and again

Six structures, one at a time.

01

One man, one angel, no witnesses

A new revelation that corrects the existing Scriptures arrives privately, to a single individual, with no second witness to the event itself. Nothing about that is impossible — but nothing about it is checkable either, and every claim below asks you to accept it on one person's word.

Mormonism

Moroni appears to Joseph Smith alone. The earliest published accounts name the angel Nephi; the First Vision has four differing firsthand versions.

Islam

Gabriel appears to Muhammad alone in the cave at Hira. The earliest account has him terrified and doubting the source, reassured by his wife and her Christian cousin.

Jehovah's Witnesses

No angel — Russell's authority came from chronology. But the organization's own appointment rests on an invisible 1919 inspection that likewise has no witnesses.

Hebrew Israelites

Not this pattern. The movement rests on a reading of Deuteronomy 28, not on a visitation.

02

The Bible had to be broken first

Before a new authority can be accepted, the existing Scriptures must be shown to be corrupted, mistranslated, or insufficient — and in every case the correction arrives from the same source as the new revelation.

Mormonism

"Many plain and precious things" removed — and Joseph's corrected Bible, which his own church never adopted.

Islam

Tahrif — but corruption of the text is a late doctrine; early Islam taught corruption of meaning.

E · The test firstConclusion leads, evidence follows Hardest to misread as mockery · shortest · the patterns become a checklist

One test, applied evenly

Start with the rule, not the comparison — because the rule is what makes this an argument rather than an insult.

Galatians 1:8

"But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed."

Paul, to the churches of Galatia — decades before any movement on this page existed

Read what it does. It doesn't say an angel couldn't appear — it grants the possibility and removes its authority. And Paul writes we: he puts himself and his own team under the same rule. That is why this is a test and not a weapon, and why it applies to anything an angel might say to you tomorrow.
