The rules every section follows

Steelman first. Every claim carries the best published defense from the faith's own scholars before any verdict is rendered. Cite their sources. Wherever possible, facts are documented from the faith's own publications. Name weak arguments as weak. Criticisms that don't survive scrutiny are marked answered — an examination that inflates weak claims forfeits the right to be believed on strong ones. Aim at the person, not the argument. The goal is never to win — it is that real people meet the real Jesus.

"But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear." — 1 Peter 3:15
Verify citations at their sources before quoting. Verdicts are editorial judgments about the state of the public argument.
