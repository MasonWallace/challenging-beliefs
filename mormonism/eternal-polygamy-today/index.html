Today's prophet is sealed to two wives

Admitted

Their own church publications admit these facts. You can make this point from LDS sources alone.

What is still practised

A widowed man may be sealed to a second wife while he is still sealed to the first. Two church presidents are in that position.

Russell M. Nelson was sealed to Wendy Watson in 2006, after Dantzel died.

Dallin H. Oaks was sealed to Kristen McMain in 2000, after June died.

Each is sealed to two women for eternity.

The part that is not equal

A living woman cannot be sealed to a second man unless the first sealing is cancelled. And D&C 132 has never been removed from the canon.

Who feels it

Carol Lynn Pearson gathered the accounts in The Ghost of Eternal Polygamy, published in 2016. They are believing Latter-day Saint women who expect to be shared in eternity.

This is grief, not a debating point.

Their answer, at full strength

Dallin H. Oaks answered it directly in General Conference in October 2019.

The church makes no doctrinal claim about how families are arranged in the next life. Trust the Lord.

Sealing tangles will be sorted out by a loving God, and nobody will be given an eternal arrangement they do not want. Since 1998, deceased women may be sealed by proxy to every man they married in life, on the assumption that she will choose.

The rule about living women is administrative. And the church has not practised plural marriage since 1904; it excommunicates those who do.

How strong is this argument?

They admit the policy. It is in the Handbook and it is public.

Oaks's answer is a candid "we don't know," and that is worth respecting. It also settles nothing.

The gap between what a widower may do and what a widow may do is still in force.
